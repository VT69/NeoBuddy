Before you start

You are signed in as you@college.edu. Nothing about you is stored yet.

What NeoBuddy keeps if you continue

  • Your name and college email from Google.
  • The identifiers you add: NeoID, and if you choose, registration number and college email.
  • Where to alert you: your ntfy topic and your email address, stored encrypted.
  • For each placement email that mentions you: the company, the date and your own row from the sheet. Your row is deleted after 180 days.

Alerts pass through the services that deliver them: ntfy.sh for push and Amazon SES for email. You can pause alerts or delete your account at any time.
