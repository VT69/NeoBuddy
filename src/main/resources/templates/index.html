How it works

  1. Sign in with your college Google account. Only students of colleges on NeoBuddy can join.
  2. Add your NeoID (and, if you like, your registration number and college email).
  3. Pick where to be told: a push notification through the free ntfy app, an email, or both.

When a shortlist or offer email mentions you, you get an alert within minutes with your own row: company, round, slot.

Your privacy, in short

  • We never ask for access to your mailbox. NeoBuddy reads the shared placement inbox only.
  • We keep your identifiers, where to alert you, and your own matched row, nothing from anyone else.
  • Your row is deleted after 180 days. Deleting your account removes everything at once.
