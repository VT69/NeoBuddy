Privacy policy

NeoBuddy is a small service run by students. It tells you when a placement email sent to your batch mentions you. This page says, in plain words, what it keeps and why.

What we keep about you

  • From Google, when you sign in: your name, your college email address and Google's id for your account. We ask Google for nothing else: no access to your mailbox, contacts or files.
  • What you add: your NeoID, and if you choose, your registration number and college email, so we can find you in shortlists.
  • Where to alert you: your ntfy push topic and your personal email address. Both are stored encrypted.
  • Your alerts: for each placement email that mentions you, the company, the type (shortlist or offer), the date, your own row from the sheet, and whether each alert was delivered.
  • When you agreed: the date you accepted this policy.

What we do not keep

  • Placement emails and their attachments. They are read once to find matches and never stored.
  • Other students' rows. You only ever see, and we only ever keep, your own.
  • Tracking or advertising data. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Who else sees it

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts NeoBuddy in Mumbai and sends alert emails (Amazon SES).
  • ntfy.sh delivers push notifications. The alert text, including your row, passes through its servers. Anyone who knows your topic can read your alerts, so keep it to yourself; you can make a new one at any time.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and the placement cell has no access to it.

How long we keep it

  • Your row from each sheet is deleted after 180 days. The entry itself (company, type, date) stays in your history until you delete your account.
  • Everything else stays until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account in Settings removes your account, identifiers, channels and history at once. Copies in our encrypted backups disappear within 30 days.
  • Server logs are kept for 30 days. They name emails by an internal id, never by their content.

One thing we cannot check

We cannot prove that a NeoID belongs to the person who adds it. Each identifier can belong to one account only. If someone has claimed yours, contact us and we will sort it out.

Your choices

  • Pause alerts or switch off a channel at any time.
  • Delete your account at any time, in Settings.
  • Questions or requests: contact.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you on your dashboard before it applies.
